# MELIO Study Universe

MELIO — Complete Website Design & Development Brief

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, frontend developer, product designer, and software architect. Your task is to design and build a complete, polished, interactive student productivity and learning platform called MELIO.

Do not create a generic productivity dashboard, a corporate SaaS interface, or an ordinary educational website. MELIO should feel like entering a personal, cozy, magical study universe.

The attached reference image is the PRIMARY visual design reference. Study its composition, colors, paper textures, typography, decorative elements, and overall atmosphere. Use its visual language consistently throughout the entire application.

1. PROJECT IDENTITY

Product name: MELIO

Brand slogan: Little Body, Big Heart.

Supporting tagline: Your Little Study Universe.

Core concept:

MELIO combines the essential tools students need into one interactive environment. It helps students organize their academic lives, manage study time, track their progress, organize learning materials, take notes, and stay motivated through gamification and a virtual pet companion.

The platform should make studying feel less stressful, more personal, creative, and enjoyable.

Target audience:

University students.

High school students.

Independent learners.

People who want to improve their study habits and productivity.

Main product philosophy:

Learning should feel rewarding.

Small achievements matter.

Productivity should not feel stressful.

Students should have a personal space for learning.

Gamification should encourage consistency, not create pressure.
